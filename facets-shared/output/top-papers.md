# Most impactful and interesting AI safety research of 2026

Ranked from the 900 in-scope candidate items for the shallow review (arXiv shortlist, notable LessWrong / Alignment Forum posts, Lenz's list of AI safety org research pages, and Safety Stack documents the scrapes missed).

**How the score works.** claude-opus-5-5 read every item and scored each 1–10 on *impact* (should it change what safety researchers believe or do?), *novelty* (new result or idea vs. incremental) and *interest* (would an expert tell colleagues?), relative to the rest of the set (2 passes averaged; Claude never saw citations or karma). *Popularity* is citations per month since publication for papers and karma for posts, as a percentile within each source. Score = 70% Claude's judgement (as a percentile) + 30% popularity, except for arXiv papers under 2 months old (they haven't had time to be cited) and org / Safety Stack items (no citations or karma collected): they're counted as average popularity (50th percentile). Treat it as a shortlist to read, not a verdict: scores come from abstracts and post openings, and no person has checked them.
